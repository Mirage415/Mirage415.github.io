Physical Time as a Missing Dimension of Robot Policy Memory Dynamics

Yushen Liang, Yue Peng, Baosheng Jin, Tianluo Zhang, Xinyu Zhang, Shuyi Zhou, Zhuoran Chen, Xinqi Liu, Shenji Wan

arXiv preprint · 2026

Paper ↗PDF ↗
Research summary

Temporal Flow Policy treats robot memory as a state that evolves over measured physical time. It combines retained history with elapsed intervals to condition diffusion and flow-matching policies, supporting timing-sensitive behavior and memory through occlusion.

↗
2026CONFERENCE

STAGE: Diagnosing Semantic Transfer at Grounded Execution in Embodied Agents

Baosheng Jin, Yushen Liang, Hua Shen

Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2026

Paper ↗PDF ↗
Research summary

STAGE examines a semantic-action gap: an embodied agent may recover the meaning of an instruction without reflecting it in its actions. SAT-Bench tests this gap with fixed-observation counterfactuals; VISA provides a lightweight interface for more instruction-consistent execution.

↗
2026WORKSHOP

TFP: Temporally Conditioned Memory-Fusion Policies for Visuomotor Learning

Yushen Liang*, Yue Peng*, Baosheng Jin*, Tianluo Zhang, Xinyu Zhang, Shuyi Zhou, Zhuoran Chen, Xinqi Liu, Shenji Wan

RSS 2026 · SemRob Workshop · Poster · * Equal contribution

Paper ↗PDF ↗Repository ↗
Research summary

TFP maintains an episode-local task-progress belief using Liquid Time-Constant dynamics and injects it into the action decoder through adaptive modulation. This lets accumulated context influence generated actions during visually ambiguous, occluded, or stage-dependent manipulation.

↗
2025PREPRINT

Do You Need Proprioceptive States in Visuomotor Policies?

Juntu Zhao, Wenbo Lu, Di Zhang, Yufeng Liu, Yushen Liang, Tianluo Zhang, Yifeng Cao, Junyuan Xie, Yingdong Hu, Shengjie Wang, Junliang Guo, Dequan Wang, Yang Gao

arXiv preprint · 2025

Paper ↗PDF ↗Project ↗
Research summary

This work investigates how proprioceptive inputs can encourage overfitting to training trajectories. State-free policies instead combine task-relevant visual observations with relative end-effector actions to improve spatial generalization and cross-embodiment adaptation.

Relation-Aware LNN-Transformer for Intersection-Centric Next-Step Prediction

Zhehong Ren, Tianluo Zhang, Yiheng Lu, Yushen Liang, Promethee Spathis

arXiv preprint · 2025

Paper ↗PDF ↗
Research summary

A road-intersection graph represents movement beyond a fixed set of points of interest. A hybrid continuous-time liquid neural network and relation-aware Transformer models temporal dynamics together with spatial dependencies for next-step location prediction.
